The Multi-dimensional Knowledge Graphs (MKG) Workshop is supported by the European Union through the Horizon Europe projects INFINITY (Grant Agreement No. 101233051) and THIRDWAVE (Grant Agreement No. 101236394).

The workshop contributes to advancing research on contextual, perspectival, and temporal knowledge representation, fostering collaboration across the Semantic Web, Knowledge Graph, and Neuro-symbolic AI communities.
