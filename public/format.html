Format & Schedule

A full-day, Dagstuhl-style ("Barstuhl") workshop: discussion-driven, not a paper track.

  1. Plenary lightning pitches · ~90 minutes
    Short position-statement pitches from all participants to surface perspectives and form breakout groups.
  2. Breakout sessions · ~180 minutes
    Three parallel groups, one per topic pillar (see Topics), with a reshuffle partway through so participants engage with more than one pillar.
  3. Alignment & synthesis plenary · 60–90 minutes
    Breakout groups report back; the room converges on shared conclusions and next steps.

Exact timings and room assignment will be finalized closer to the workshop day (25 or 26 October 2026, assigned by the ISWC chairs).
