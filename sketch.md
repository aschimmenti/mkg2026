## 4. Website — required + recommended content

**Officially required:** emphasize the **open, interactive, discussion-driven** nature; highlight **(a) key challenges/topics, (b) intended format, (c) expected outcomes**.

Recommended full structure (all content is in your proposal unless flagged):

- [ ]  **Header** — title, acronym, "Full-day Dagstuhl-Style Workshop @ ISWC 2026, Bari, 25–26 Oct 2026." One line: *discussion-driven, not a paper track.*
- [ ]  **About** (~150–200 words) — reuse the abstract; state clearly there are no formal paper presentations.
- [ ]  **Topics & challenges** — the three interconnected challenges (formal models for multi-dimensional assertions; scalable query/storage/indexing; benchmarks & use cases in DH / Cultural Heritage / Life Sciences). Optionally list RQ1–3 and EC1–3 as recruitment bait.
- [ ]  **Format & schedule** — the section chairs care most about: 90-min plenary lightning pitches → 180-min breakouts (3 pillars, with reshuffling) → 60–90-min alignment & synthesis plenary.
- [ ]  **Expected outcomes** — manifesto/framework doc → MKG White Paper → possible Dagstuhl seminar; dissemination via website, Zenodo, and/or open repo.
- [ ]  **Call for participation** ⚠️ *needs a decision* — open call for lightning-pitch / position statements: who should submit, format, length, **submission channel** (email / EasyChair / form), and a **deadline** (suggest 2–4 weeks before 25 Oct). State plainly: **open to all ISWC attendees**, no submission required to attend.
- [ ]  **Organizers** — the 5 chairs with affiliations, homepages, one-line bios (already written in the proposal). Highlight the junior/senior + multi-institution mix.
- [ ]  **Important dates** — pitch-statement deadline, notification (if you curate), workshop day.
- [ ]  **Registration & attendance** — link to ISWC registration; note attendance requires a Pre-conference Days ticket (250 € add-on, or 350 € standalone), +VAT.
